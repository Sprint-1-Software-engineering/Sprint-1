# Problem validation

**Status: interviews not conducted.** The team confirmed this on 5 November 2026.

We hypothesize that tutors and students lose track of lesson changes and homework when using separate tools. We do not have a measured baseline or validated frequency yet.

## Published context

[OECD TALIS 2024](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/results-from-talis-2024_90df6235-en/full-report/the-demands-of-teaching_0e941e2f.html) reports administrative workload as a source of stress for about half of teachers across OECD education systems. The population is school teachers. We cannot treat it as a tutor-specific baseline or proof of calendar problems.

## Planned interviews

Owners: Toprak and Arda. Speak to at least two teachers and two students if available. This is our validation plan, not a course-mandated sample size.

Ask:
1. How do you arrange a lesson now?
2. How often does a lesson time change? What happened last time?
3. How do you send or find homework?
4. What gets lost or takes extra time?
5. Would the proposed workflow fit your current practice?

Record only real findings. Ask permission before recording and avoid names or private student details in the repository.
